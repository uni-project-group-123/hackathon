# Hackathon Brief

# Brief Idea
Safety and Security on Campus: Proactive and Intelligent Safety Systems
An application to authenticate students on campus. Currently to access your labs, you
are using your ID cards, create a system to ensure the right people are entering the
labs -This could be using facial recognition or any other method of verification you
think suitable (or multiple!). This app should be able to contact security and keep
them in-the-loop about building usage. The application could also give indicators to
total users people in the building and understand when people enter and leave
Smart ID verification system, suspicious behaviour alerts and detection, safe and at
night route planning, alarms and panic options.
